# Illustration Style — Warm Folk Reverie

> **Selected production direction:** Variant B — Painted Folk Sculpture. Use colour intensity 4/5, handmade texture 4/5, stylisation 4/5, pattern density 4/5, whimsy 4/5, nostalgia 3/5 and dimensionality 3/5 as the current object-and-character target.

## Core definition

**Warm Folk Reverie** is a hand-drawn storybook style inspired by Mexican folk craft and the joyful, reflective atmosphere of Día de Muertos. Forms are simple, rounded and full of personality; surfaces feel painted rather than rendered. Rich colour is softened with warmth, so even turquoise, magenta and green feel sun-faded, earthy and companionable rather than neon.

The work should feel as if it belongs to a treasured illustrated book: tactile, slightly imperfect, whimsical and nostalgic. Brush edges, pencil marks, dry-brush grain and paper texture remain visible. Pattern is used with intention—painted florals, dots, stepped diamonds, leaf shapes and carved lines follow the form of an object or creature instead of covering everything evenly.

Mexican references should be specific and respectful. Draw from materials and visual language such as painted wood and papier-mâché alebrijes, Oaxacan carving traditions, cempasúchil petals, papel picado, hand-painted ceramics, embroidery-like florals and regionally grounded geometric motifs. Avoid generic “tribal” styling, costume-like ceremonial dress, sacred symbols used as decoration, and direct imitation of a living artist’s work.

## The visual recipe

- **Shape:** clear silhouettes, soft geometry, gentle exaggeration and a little asymmetry.
- **Line:** lively dark-brown or coloured linework; varied pressure; never a perfectly clean vector outline.
- **Paint:** opaque gouache and coloured-pencil character with dry-brush transitions, scuffs and overlaps.
- **Light:** diffuse golden light with restrained highlights; no glossy 3D plastic finish.
- **Depth:** mostly flat-to-shallow dimensionality, with simple overlapping shapes and soft painted shadows.
- **Palette:** warm, earthy jewel tones on cream, sand or transparent grounds.
- **Pattern:** a few large motifs supported by small marks; pattern density follows anatomy and material.
- **Mood:** affectionate, curious, celebratory and safe; magic without menace.

## Style scale

Use each axis from **1 to 5**. The recommended house style is shown in the final column.

| Axis | 1 | 3 | 5 | House target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Colour intensity** | dusty neutrals | muted folk colour | electric festival colour | **3.5** — colourful, warmed and slightly muted |
| **Handmade texture** | smooth digital | visible brush and pencil | rough sketch / heavy grain | **4** — clearly handmade, still legible at UI size |
| **Stylisation** | natural proportions | simplified storybook forms | extreme caricature | **3.5** — distinctive but emotionally readable |
| **Pattern density** | nearly plain | patterned focal zones | every surface decorated | **3** — breathing room around intentional motifs |
| **Whimsy** | observational | playful exaggeration | surreal comedy | **3.5** — magical and warm, never silly or chaotic |
| **Nostalgia** | crisp contemporary | softened print character | heavily aged antique | **3.5** — heirloom warmth without faux damage |
| **Dimensionality** | flat cut-paper | shallow painted volume | glossy cinematic 3D | **2.5** — illustrative volume, matte surfaces |
| **Cultural specificity** | generic fantasy | recognisable craft language | dense literal references | **4** — grounded motifs, selectively applied |

### Product range

- **UI-small (icons and objects):** reduce handmade texture to 3 and pattern density to 2.5; keep the silhouette unmistakable.
- **Character art:** use the full house targets; keep eyes and gesture emotionally readable.
- **Memory vignettes:** raise nostalgia to 4 and lower colour intensity to 3 for a quieter, recollected feeling.
- **Celebration moments:** raise colour intensity and whimsy to 4 while keeping the warm colour cast.

## Colour direction

Build palettes around warm cream and earthen darks, then select four to six accents:

- cempasúchil orange and saffron
- cochineal red and dusty magenta
- bougainvillea pink
- agave teal and oxidised turquoise
- cactus and avocado green
- cornflower or indigo blue
- cacao brown and warm charcoal for outlines

Avoid pure digital primaries, cold blue-white highlights, large fields of true black, and rainbow colour distributed with equal intensity. A warm ochre or terracotta undertone should connect the palette.

## Applying it consistently

### Objects

Preserve the object’s instantly recognisable silhouette. Show its material through a few tactile marks—ceramic brushwork, woven fibres, carved wood, worn enamel—then place patterns only on natural panels, rims or seams. Give objects a slight lean or uneven contour so they feel loved and handled.

### People

Use simplified, expressive proportions and natural gestures. Let clothing, place and everyday objects carry identity rather than stereotypes. Faces should be warm and individual, not doll-like or hyper-rendered. In this app, never generate the person being remembered; their real photograph remains unaltered.

### Animals and alebrijes

Begin with one dominant animal so the silhouette reads at a glance. Add two or three secondary-animal traits at clear anatomical junctions. Patterns should curve with fur, feathers, scales and limbs. The creature’s face and posture should communicate a kind, curious guide—not a monster, mascot, or pet from an existing film.

## Alebrije construction rule

**One base + three accents + one personality gesture.**

For the first guide:

- **Base:** alpaca—long neck, soft muzzle, fluffy chest and alert ears.
- **Accent 1:** broad bird wings, shaped for a readable silhouette.
- **Accent 2:** long ostrich legs with two-toed feet.
- **Accent 3:** cockatiel crest and a small sweeping feather tail.
- **Gesture:** a gentle forward lean, as though listening to a story.

Keep the joins believable but visibly fantastical. The alpaca remains the first read; the borrowed traits are accents, not a collage of unrelated parts.

## Do / avoid

**Do:** tactile paint, warm paper, uneven edges, asymmetry, clear silhouettes, purposeful pattern, grounded Mexican craft references, affectionate expressions and quiet visual humour.

**Avoid:** photorealism, glossy 3D rendering, neon rainbow overload, generic pan-Indigenous imagery, skull faces on people, horror imagery, Halloween symbols, over-detailed backgrounds, perfect vector symmetry, imitation of copyrighted film characters, and direct copying of a specific artist.

## Reusable prompt kernel

> Hand-drawn storybook illustration in the Warm Folk Reverie house style: matte gouache and coloured pencil on warm cream paper, visible brush grain and imperfect coloured outlines, rounded simplified forms, shallow painted volume, warm muted Mexican folk palette, selective painted florals and stepped geometric patterns that follow the form, affectionate and slightly whimsical, nostalgic without looking distressed, culturally grounded and never stereotypical. No photorealism, glossy 3D finish, neon rainbow, horror, Halloween imagery, text or watermark.
